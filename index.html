Thresholds used on this page are the unit's reporting benchmarks, editable in THRESHOLDS at the top of the page script: single-pool Kt/V ≥ 1.2, effective time ≥ 240 min, blood flow ≥ 250 ml/min, UF rate ≤ 1000 ml/h, post-dialysis systolic 90–180 mmHg. They flag sessions for a human to look at — they are not a clinical assessment, and every flag needs review against the patient's prescription and notes.

The score is a triage aid for picking the next chart to open, not a clinical assessment and not a comparison between patients. It combines four delivery measures the unit controls, so a low score usually points at prescription, vascular access or dialyser choice rather than at the patient. Body size, residual function and comorbidity all move Kt/V independently of how well a session ran, and every row here needs reading against the patient's own prescription before anything changes.
